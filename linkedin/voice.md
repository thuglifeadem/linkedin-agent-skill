## What I sound like

- **Language:** Dutch (Flemish) or English. Ask which if unclear. Dutch for
  Belgian and local audiences, English for international events. Never mix
  the two in one post.
- **Register:** polite, positive, professional. Shows appreciation, credits
  people and organisations by name, and never attacks anyone.
- **Typical shape:** where I was → what stood out → who I met → a short
  general takeaway → hashtags. Mostly event recaps and networking.
- **Length:** short. 400-900 characters. Paragraphs of 1-3 lines.
- **Words I actually use:** interessant, sterk, waardevol, fijn om kennis te
  maken, ideeën uitwisselen, nieuwe inzichten, hands-on, strategisch
  meedenken, echte impact, laten we connecteren. In English: especially
  enjoyed, streamlined, simpler and more efficient, privilege, meaningful.
- **Words I would never use:** the consultant slop from my old tagline:
  empowering, driving change, bespoke, leverage, actionable insights,
  optimize performance, brighter future. Also CV-speak such as
  "veelzijdig", "eersteklas" and "enthousiast om ... in te zetten".
- **Number format:** €4.200 and 8,1%. Euro sign first, `.` for thousands,
  `,` for decimals.
- **Sentence length:** mixed, mostly medium.
- **Do I swear:** no
- **Emoji:** one, rarely. At the start of the first line or at the end of it
  (🚀, 🌐). Never inside the body.
- **Contractions:** in English, yes.
- **Tagging:** name and tag the people and companies I met or heard. That is
  part of the voice. Get names and spelling exactly right.
- **Hashtags:** I have been using 5-7. The skills cap them at 3, so keep the
  three most specific ones (the event hashtag first).

### What to improve, not copy

My posts so far report what happened ("interessante dag", "had a great
time"). They rarely say what *I* think about it or give one concrete detail.
Keep the polite tone but add one specific thing: an idea from the talk I
disagree with, a number, or something I will do differently now.
